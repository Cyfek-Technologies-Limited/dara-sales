# Automatic prospect discovery

Apply migrations with `pnpm db:migrate:deploy`, then deploy the application. The existing authenticated `/api/cron/email-outreach` scheduler now runs an email worker and schedules a prospect discovery batch using Next's `after()` lifecycle. Keep the existing Bearer CRON_SECRET header and every-minute schedule. Web research continues after the scheduler response to avoid its request timeout; server maxDuration is 180 seconds. The `?check=1` connection check remains read-only and runs neither worker. Research failures are recorded in discovery status on the Prospects page rather than in the scheduler's already-returned response.

On the Prospects page, add up to 20 target locations, optionally choose a role, and enable discovery once. Location names may contain commas (for example Lagos, Nigeria); each location has its own field. Empty entries and case-insensitive duplicates are removed on save. Existing single-location settings are preserved. No manual business search is required afterward. Each category is searched across all locations before moving to the next category, repeating continuously. The daily target and research budget cover all locations combined. Staff are limited to currently assigned industries and new records belong to them; administrators search configured industries and save unassigned records. Settings and progress are private to each member. Pausing fences already-running work before it saves.

Each run asks for up to 5 businesses and saves only sourced businesses with a public email or phone. It skips existing organization records by company name or website host, does not reassign them, and only fills blank contact fields on duplicates the member can access. Official company pages establish identity and contacts; public LinkedIn person profiles can establish role matches and explicitly published work channels. Public social pages can aid discovery but contact details require company or named-profile evidence. No account login, private scraping or email-pattern guessing is used.

Discovery runs at most once per member every 30 minutes, with at most 30 attempts per UTC database day, and a daily target of 50 or 100 newly saved prospects. These are ceilings, not guarantees. Exhausted daily budgets reset on the next scheduler tick on a new day. Errors back off one hour. Leases and row locks prevent overlapping discovery batches, expired workers cannot save, and organization locks serialize automatic deduplication. Recent saved businesses are excluded from subsequent research prompts. Limits are per enabled member and API usage is billed by the configured provider.

The existing email queue includes newly saved prospects only under the organization's already-approved outreach configuration. Enabling discovery does not approve or start a draft email campaign. Existing email rate limits and suppression checks remain in force.

Deployment alone leaves discovery paused. Enable it for each member who wants ongoing searches. Ensure OPENAI_API_KEY, the existing CRON_SECRET, and the production scheduler are configured. Neither the page's 3-second status refresh nor an open browser is required for background work. The activity panel reports durable search, contact-check and save stages, the current company, and completed contact checks. Daily progress counts committed new prospects only; it does not estimate completion while web research is running. Apply the discovery-progress migration before deploying the reporting worker.
